# Chief Architect

Purpose:

- owns the overall architecture capability, direction, coherence, and delivery across the project or organization scope

Primary responsibilities:

- define and oversee the architecture approach, operating model, and expected outcomes
- coordinate architecture work across enterprise, solution, data, integration, domain, technology, and governance roles
- resolve major tradeoffs, conflicts, and cross-cutting design decisions
- ensure architecture artifacts are complete, coherent, and aligned to business goals
- oversee architecture governance, quality, and delivery effectiveness

Typical outputs:

- `architecture-vision`
- `principle`
- `technology-standard`
- `decision`
- `roadmap`
- `compliance-assessment`
- `risk`
- coordination summaries, review-gate decisions, and cross-skill handoff direction

Key collaboration points:

- with enterprise architects on target state and strategic architecture direction
- with solution architects on solution coherence and delivery alignment
- with architecture governance leads on review quality, conformance, and exceptions
- with all specialist architects on scope, priorities, and architectural accountability
