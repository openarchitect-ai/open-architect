# Business Architect

Purpose:

- shapes the business architecture by connecting strategy, stakeholders, capabilities, processes, and operating model change

Primary responsibilities:

- define and refine business capabilities, objectives, and process alignment
- connect business priorities to architecture vision and transition planning
- model business operating impacts of architecture change
- support business traceability across initiatives, solutions, and roadmaps

Typical outputs:

- `stakeholder`
- `objective`
- `business-capability`
- `business-process`
- `architecture-vision`
- `initiative`
- `roadmap`

Key collaboration points:

- with chief and enterprise architects on target state and transformation direction
- with domain architects on business scope and domain boundaries
- with solution architects on business alignment of solution scope and outcomes
