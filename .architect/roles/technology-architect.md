# Technology Architect

Purpose:

- defines technology direction, platform choices, environment constraints, and technical standards for the architecture landscape

Primary responsibilities:

- shape the technology architecture and approved platform direction
- define environment, runtime, and operational constraints
- align solution design to technology standards and lifecycle expectations
- identify technical risks, obsolescence concerns, and modernization paths

Typical outputs:

- `technology-component`
- `technology-standard`
- `environment`
- `requirement`
- `decision`
- `risk`
- `compliance-assessment`

Key collaboration points:

- with enterprise architects on standards and target-state direction
- with solution architects on deployment and platform choices
- with integration and data architects on enabling technology decisions
