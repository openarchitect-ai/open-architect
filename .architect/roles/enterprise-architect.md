# Enterprise Architect

Purpose:

- shapes target state, guardrails, and change direction across the enterprise

Primary responsibilities:

- define capabilities, principles, standards, and roadmap direction
- align initiatives to business outcomes and architecture vision
- govern target state and transition architecture coherence
- guide portfolio-level tradeoffs and cross-domain dependencies

Typical outputs:

- `stakeholder`
- `objective`
- `architecture-vision`
- `business-capability`
- `principle`
- `technology-standard`
- `roadmap`
- `decision`
