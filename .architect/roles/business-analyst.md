# Business Analyst

Purpose:

- elicits, refines, and manages business and functional requirements while maintaining traceability between stakeholder needs, business processes, and solution scope

Primary responsibilities:

- gather and clarify stakeholder needs, business rules, and functional expectations
- define and refine business and functional requirements
- maintain traceability from stakeholder concerns and objectives to requirements, processes, and solution scope
- support change impact analysis and requirement quality throughout architecture and delivery work

Typical outputs:

- `stakeholder`
- `objective`
- `business-process`
- `requirement`
- `initiative`
- `compliance-assessment`

Key collaboration points:

- with business architects on business context, process alignment, and capability outcomes
- with solution architects on requirement interpretation and solution scope
- with enterprise architects on alignment to objectives and architecture vision
- with architecture governance leads on requirement evidence and traceability quality
