# Solution Architect

Purpose:

- designs bounded solutions that satisfy business intent and delivery constraints

Primary responsibilities:

- define end-to-end solution scope and architecture
- align design to principles, standards, and requirements
- model applications, services, interfaces, data, and environments
- support transition planning, delivery traceability, and implementation governance

Typical outputs:

- `solution`
- `requirement`
- `application`
- `application-service`
- `interface`
- `data-object`
- `environment`
- `transition-architecture`
- `work-package`
- `compliance-assessment`
- `risk`
