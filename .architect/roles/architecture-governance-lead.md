# Architecture Governance Lead

Purpose:

- governs architecture quality, compliance, decision-making, and change control across the architecture capability

Primary responsibilities:

- run architecture review and compliance processes
- ensure decisions, standards, and principles are applied consistently
- track waivers, risks, and remediation actions
- maintain governance cadence and architecture evidence quality

Typical outputs:

- `decision`
- `compliance-assessment`
- `risk`
- `principle`
- `technology-standard`
- `requirement`

Key collaboration points:

- with enterprise architects on policy and target-state governance
- with solution architects on design conformance
- with delivery leads on remediation and exception handling
