# Domain Architect

Purpose:

- shapes architecture direction, coherence, and decision-making within a bounded business or technology domain

Primary responsibilities:

- translate enterprise direction into domain-specific architecture
- maintain domain views across capability, process, application, data, and change
- identify domain risks, dependencies, and modernization priorities
- support governance and consistency across solutions in the domain

Typical outputs:

- `stakeholder`
- `objective`
- `business-capability`
- `application`
- `data-object`
- `initiative`
- `roadmap`
- `risk`

Key collaboration points:

- with enterprise architects on alignment to target state and principles
- with solution architects on domain solution design choices
- with product or delivery teams on domain transition planning
