# DORA

## Regulation

- Regulation (EU) 2022/2554
- ESMA overview: https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/digital-operational-resilience-act-dora
- Applies from January 17, 2025

## Architecture Relevance

DORA matters for EU financial entities and their ICT risk, resilience,
incident-reporting, testing, and third-party dependency architecture.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- ICT risk management and operational resilience
- third-party and critical provider dependency visibility
- incident response and reporting support
- resilience, failover, and recovery expectations
- auditability and control evidence

## Useful Patterns

- `multi-region-failover`
- `shared-service-slo-operating-model`
- `rollback-readiness-gate`
- `cost-allocation-and-showback`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
