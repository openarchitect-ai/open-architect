# EU Data Act

## Regulation

- Regulation (EU) 2023/2854
- Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854

## Architecture Relevance

The EU Data Act matters when the architecture involves connected products,
related services, data access rights, cloud switching, or interoperability
obligations that affect how data is exposed and reused.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- data access and sharing obligations
- interoperability and portability expectations
- switching and lock-in reduction for cloud or data-processing services
- producer and consumer rights around generated data
- contractual and technical controls for data availability

## Useful Patterns

- `data-contract-governance`
- `data-sharing-agreement-governance`
- `cross-boundary-data-sharing-controls`
- `data-lineage-and-provenance`
- `master-reference-data-synchronization`
