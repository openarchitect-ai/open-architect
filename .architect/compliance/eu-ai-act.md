# EU AI Act

## Regulation

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
- Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/
- Commission overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- Entered into force on August 1, 2024

## Architecture Relevance

The EU AI Act matters when a project develops, procures, integrates, or uses AI
systems in ways that may fall into prohibited, high-risk, or transparency-
related categories.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- identifying whether AI is in scope at all
- high-risk AI classification and use-case boundaries
- model, data, and decision traceability
- human oversight and governance
- logging, evidence, and post-deployment monitoring
- supplier and third-party responsibility splits
