# NIS2

## Regulation

- Directive (EU) 2022/2555
- Commission overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive
- Entered into force on January 16, 2023

## Architecture Relevance

NIS2 matters when the project supports an entity in a covered critical or
important sector and cybersecurity governance, resilience, incident handling,
and supplier risk materially affect the solution.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- cybersecurity governance and risk management
- incident handling and reporting readiness
- supply-chain and third-party dependencies
- identity, segmentation, and operational resilience
- evidence of controls and review

## Useful Patterns

- `zero-trust-network-segmentation`
- `security-control-inheritance`
- `secrets-breach-response`
- `reference-architecture-conformance-review`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
