# Life Sciences and Pharma Profile

Use this profile when the architecture supports regulated research, product
development, manufacturing, quality, pharmacovigilance, clinical processes, or
other life-sciences and pharmaceutical operations.

## Why It Matters

Life-sciences architectures often need stronger traceability, evidence,
validation, records handling, controlled access, and change assurance because
product quality, patient safety, and regulated operations can depend directly
on the system behavior.

## Commonly Relevant Regulation Families

- healthcare and privacy obligations relevant to the operating jurisdiction
- product, quality, or validation expectations in regulated life-sciences
  environments
- records, audit, and controlled-change obligations

## Architecture-Relevant Questions

- Does the system support regulated product, research, quality, or safety
  workflows?
- Are records, approvals, and controlled changes strong enough to support
  validation and audit?
- Are data lineage, retention, and evidence capture explicit?
- Do third-party labs, platforms, or manufacturing dependencies shape risk?

## Architecture Implications

- Treat traceability, validation, records, and approval flows as part of the
  architecture.
- Keep quality-sensitive interfaces, governed data movement, and evidence
  boundaries visible.
- Expect stronger review requirements around change control, audit, and access
  decisions.
