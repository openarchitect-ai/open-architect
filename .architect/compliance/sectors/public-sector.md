# Public Sector Profile

Use this profile when the architecture supports government agencies, public
administration, publicly funded services, or systems that face stronger public
accountability, procurement, records, sovereignty, and assurance expectations.

## Why It Matters

Public-sector architectures often need more explicit governance, auditability,
records handling, accessibility, supplier scrutiny, and service continuity than
 a typical commercial solution.

## Commonly Relevant Regulation Families

- public-sector procurement or assurance obligations
- privacy and records obligations relevant to the operating jurisdiction
- cybersecurity and incident-reporting obligations where critical services are
  involved

## Architecture-Relevant Questions

- Does the solution support public-service delivery, regulated recordkeeping, or
  public accountability obligations?
- Are sovereignty, residency, supplier dependency, or audit-evidence
  expectations stronger than in a typical private-sector deployment?
- Does the solution need higher assurance around accessibility, continuity, or
  decision traceability?

## Architecture Implications

- Treat records, audit trails, evidence retention, and approval history as
  first-class architecture concerns.
- Keep supplier dependency, service continuity, and sovereignty assumptions
  visible.
- Expect stricter review involvement from governance, security, and compliance
  roles.
