# GDPR

## Regulation

- General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679
- Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

## Architecture Relevance

GDPR matters when the project processes personal data relating to individuals in
the EU or EEA, especially when architecture choices affect data minimization,
purpose limitation, retention, cross-border transfer, data subject rights,
security, or processor relationships.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- personal data classification and minimization
- controller and processor boundaries
- lawful basis, purpose limitation, and retention support
- cross-border transfer and residency implications
- auditability and access control
- breach response and evidence

## Useful Patterns

- `data-classification-and-handling`
- `regulated-data-access-approval`
- `regulated-data-retention-and-legal-hold`
- `data-lineage-and-provenance`
- `cross-boundary-data-sharing-controls`
