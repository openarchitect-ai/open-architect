# MiCA

## Regulation

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1114
- Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex:32023R1114

## Architecture Relevance

MiCA matters when the project involves crypto-assets, issuers, or crypto-asset
service providers and architecture choices affect custody, resilience,
governance, or regulatory reporting.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- operational resilience and incident handling
- custody, segregation, and privileged access
- governance, evidence, and reporting support
- third-party dependencies and service-provider controls
- data and transaction traceability

## Useful Patterns

- `shared-service-slo-operating-model`
- `cutover-command-center`
- `privileged-session-monitoring`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
- `zero-trust-network-segmentation`
