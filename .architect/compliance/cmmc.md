# CMMC

## Regulation

- DCSA CMMC overview:
  https://www.dcsa.mil/Industrial-Security/Controlled-Unclassified-Information-CUI/Cybersecurity-Maturity-Model-Certification-CMMC/

## Architecture Relevance

CMMC matters when the architecture supports defense-industry workloads or
systems that process controlled unclassified information and need stronger proof
of cybersecurity maturity, boundary control, evidence, and supplier discipline.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- handling and segregation of controlled unclassified information
- identity, access, and privileged activity control
- audit, monitoring, and evidence retention
- managed service and supplier dependency control
- configuration discipline and operational security proof

## Useful Patterns

- `security-control-inheritance`
- `zero-trust-network-segmentation`
- `privileged-access-segregation`
- `platform-tenancy-isolation`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
