# SEC Cyber Disclosure Rules

## Regulation

- SEC press release: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-139
- Final rule page: https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2023/07/s7-09-22
- Adopted on July 26, 2023

## Architecture Relevance

These rules matter for public companies where cyber incident materiality,
governance, risk management, and evidence readiness affect disclosure
obligations.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- incident detection and materiality support
- governance and decision escalation
- evidence trail for cyber events and controls
- board or executive reporting support

## Useful Patterns

- `architecture-board-decision-escalation`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
- `decision-evidence-packaging`
- `secrets-breach-response`
- `cutover-command-center`
