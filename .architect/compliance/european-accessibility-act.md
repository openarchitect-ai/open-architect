# European Accessibility Act

## Regulation

- European Accessibility Act overview:
  https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/european-accessibility-act-eaa_en

## Architecture Relevance

The European Accessibility Act matters for digital products and services sold in
the EU where accessibility requirements shape customer journeys, service
channels, payment experiences, communication flows, and self-service
interactions.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- accessible digital channels and customer journeys
- support for banking, e-commerce, communications, and self-service flows
- accessible identity, payment, and notification steps
- content and service design that does not depend on inaccessible side paths
- evidence that accessibility is part of the service design, not a late overlay
