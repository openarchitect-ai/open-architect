# CCPA / CPRA

## Regulation

- California DOJ CCPA page: https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa
- California rights overview: https://privacy.ca.gov/california-privacy-rights/rights-under-the-california-consumer-privacy-act/

## Architecture Relevance

CCPA and CPRA matter when the project handles personal information of
California residents and architecture choices affect collection, sharing,
retention, access, and deletion support.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- personal information classification
- purpose and sharing boundaries
- retention and deletion support
- access and auditability
- service provider and contractor boundaries

## Useful Patterns

- `data-classification-and-handling`
- `regulated-data-access-approval`
- `regulated-data-retention-and-legal-hold`
- `data-lineage-and-provenance`
- `data-sharing-agreement-governance`
