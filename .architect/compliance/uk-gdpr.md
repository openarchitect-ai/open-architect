# UK GDPR

## Regulation

- UK GDPR guidance: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/
- Guide to data protection: https://ico.org.uk/media/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/guide-to-the-general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr-1-1.pdf

## Architecture Relevance

UK GDPR matters when the project processes personal data in the United Kingdom
or serves UK users in ways that affect lawful processing, transparency,
retention, rights handling, data protection by design, security, or
cross-border processing.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- lawful basis, transparency, and accountability support
- data protection by design and by default
- rights handling, deletion, and correction support
- retention and evidence of compliant processing
- processor and supplier boundaries
- cross-border transfer and UK-specific governance
