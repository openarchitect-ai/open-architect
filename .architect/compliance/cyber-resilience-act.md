# Cyber Resilience Act

## Regulation

- Regulation (EU) 2024/2847
- Official text: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj/eng
- Commission overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act

## Architecture Relevance

The Cyber Resilience Act matters when the project builds, supplies, or
significantly changes hardware or software products with digital elements for
the EU market and product-level cybersecurity requirements shape design,
support, or update strategy.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- secure-by-design product expectations
- vulnerability handling and coordinated remediation
- product update and support lifecycle
- software bill of materials and supply-chain visibility
- logging, reporting, and evidence of product security controls

## Useful Patterns

- `runtime-policy-enforcement`
- `shared-platform-guardrail-model`
- `secrets-breach-response`
- `environment-drift-detection`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
