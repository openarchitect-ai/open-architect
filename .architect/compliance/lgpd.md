# LGPD

## Regulation

- Brazil LGPD portal: https://www.gov.br/pt-br/lgpd
- ANPD portal: https://www.gov.br/anpd

## Architecture Relevance

LGPD matters when the project processes personal data in Brazil or serves
Brazilian users in ways that affect lawful processing, privacy rights, security,
retention, data sharing, or cross-border transfer.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- personal data classification and lawful processing support
- rights handling, deletion, and correction support
- security and evidence around personal-data processing
- controller, operator, and third-party boundaries
- cross-border transfer and disclosure governance

## Useful Patterns

- `data-classification-and-handling`
- `data-sharing-agreement-governance`
- `data-lineage-and-provenance`
- `regulated-data-access-approval`
- `architecture-assurance-evidence-trail`
