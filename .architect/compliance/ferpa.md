# FERPA

## Regulation

- FERPA overview: https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa

## Architecture Relevance

FERPA matters when the architecture handles student education records for U.S.
educational institutions and design choices affect access, disclosure,
retention, and third-party handling.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- education-record access boundaries
- disclosure controls and role-based access
- auditability of record access and sharing
- service-provider and school-official relationships
- retention and stewardship of student records

## Useful Patterns

- `data-domain-ownership`
- `regulated-data-access-approval`
- `data-sharing-agreement-governance`
- `audit-and-tamper-evident-logging`
- `policy-to-control-traceability`
