# eIDAS / European Digital Identity Framework

## Regulation

- eIDAS overview: https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eidas-regulation
- European Digital Identity Framework amendment: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj

## Architecture Relevance

eIDAS matters when the project depends on electronic identification, trust
services, signatures, seals, wallets, or cross-border identity trust inside the
EU.

## Typical Architecture Concerns

- identity assurance and trust levels
- electronic signatures and trust services
- wallet and identity-provider integration
- cross-border identity interoperability
- auditability of identity assertions and trust events

## Useful Patterns

- `identity-federation`
- `workload-identity-federation`
- `privileged-session-monitoring`
- `data-lineage-and-provenance`
- `runtime-policy-enforcement`
