# Regulatory Controls

Use this folder for mappings from regulations to architecture-relevant control
themes.

Examples:

- identity and access management
- incident reporting and response
- data retention and legal hold
- data classification and cross-border transfer
- third-party risk and resilience
- logging, audit, and evidence retention

These notes complement patterns and compliance-assessment artifacts by making it
easier to move from legal obligation to architecture action.
