# Notes

One short paragraph per heading. Keep it tight — these are seeds, not
formal artifacts. Replace or remove headings that don't apply.

## Business goal

What is this engagement trying to achieve, in business terms?

## Current problem

What hurts today? What is the pain motivating the work?

## In-scope systems

Which applications / domains / data are in scope?

## Out-of-scope

What is explicitly *not* in scope?

## Stakeholders

Who has decision rights or strong opinions? Who do we need to align with?

## Constraints

Budget, deadline, regulatory, vendor, technical constraints.

## Questions for the architect / assistant

The first things to ask, investigate, or confirm.
