# Decision Evidence Packaging

## Purpose

Prepare architecture decisions with a clear, reviewable evidence pack so
approvers can assess tradeoffs, assumptions, and impacts without relying on
fragmented discussion history.

## When To Use

- significant decisions need formal review or approval
- multiple options and tradeoffs must be compared clearly
- governance forums need repeatable decision preparation

## When Not To Use

- the choice is minor and can be resolved safely within normal local authority
- evidence is so incomplete that packaging would hide the real uncertainty

## Assumptions

- relevant sources and impacts can be assembled
- decision-makers need concise but structured context
- the evidence pack will be maintained as the decision evolves

## Key Components

- decision statement
- option summary and tradeoffs
- evidence, references, and assumptions
- impacts, risks, and recommendation

## Benefits

- improves decision quality and review efficiency
- reduces reliance on informal memory or meeting context
- supports better traceability from decision to rationale

## Tradeoffs

- adds preparation effort
- can feel heavyweight for smaller decisions
- requires discipline to keep evidence current

## Common Risks

- evidence packs becoming biased toward a preferred option
- missing impacts hidden behind concise packaging
- approvals given without challenging assumptions properly
