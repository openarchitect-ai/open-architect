# Architecture Board Decision Escalation

## Purpose

Escalate architecture decisions deliberately to the right governance forum when
scope, risk, cost, or cross-domain impact exceeds normal local decision rights.

## When To Use

- a decision crosses domain or platform boundaries
- local teams cannot resolve a tradeoff within their mandate
- governance, risk, or investment impact needs broader authority

## When Not To Use

- the issue can be resolved safely within normal team or role authority
- escalation would be used as a substitute for doing local analysis first

## Assumptions

- escalation thresholds can be defined
- architecture board or equivalent authority exists
- pre-escalation evidence can be assembled clearly

## Key Components

- escalation trigger criteria
- evidence and options packet
- decision forum and authority
- post-decision communication and follow-through

## Benefits

- improves clarity on who decides what
- reduces unresolved cross-team architecture disputes
- supports stronger governance of high-impact choices

## Tradeoffs

- adds time and process overhead
- can slow urgent work if thresholds are vague
- requires board capacity and disciplined preparation

## Common Risks

- escalating too early or too often
- poor evidence quality reaching the board
- escalated decisions not being reflected back into artifacts and plans
