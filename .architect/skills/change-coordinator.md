# Change Coordinator

## Purpose

Coordinate architecture changes from identified impact through decision,
transition planning, delivery follow-up, and governance tracking.

## Use When

- approved or emerging changes affect architecture scope or direction
- requirement changes need structured impact routing
- delivery and governance teams need a single coordination view of architecture change

## Inputs

- changed or newly approved requirements
- impacted architecture artifacts
- open decisions, gaps, risks, and work packages

## Outputs

- coordinated updates across `decision`, `gap`, `transition-architecture`, `work-package`, and related artifacts
- explicit impact paths and follow-up ownership
- structured change status for architecture governance and delivery review

## Typical Collaboration

- works closely with `chief-architect` and `architecture-governance-lead`
- supports `transition-planner`, `risk-compliance-assessor`, `communication-packager`, and `architecture-review`
