# Artifact Maintainer

## Purpose

Keep architecture documents, diagrams, and related artifacts aligned when
requirements, decisions, risks, or transition plans change over time.

## Use When

- architecture outputs drift out of sync after project changes
- diagrams or documents need refresh after decision updates
- governance findings require coordinated updates across multiple outputs

## Inputs

- changed requirements, decisions, risks, work packages, or transition artifacts
- existing documents, diagrams, and linked architecture content

## Outputs

- synchronized updates across model, documents, diagrams, and supporting outputs
- identified stale artifacts and required follow-up actions
- improved consistency across architecture deliverables

## Typical Collaboration

- works closely with `change-coordinator`, `architecture-documenter`, and `diagram-author`
- supports long-running project execution and governance follow-up
