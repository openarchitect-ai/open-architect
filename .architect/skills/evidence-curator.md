# Evidence Curator

## Purpose

Collect and organize the references, source links, workshop outcomes, and
rationale that support architecture artifacts and governance decisions.

## Use When

- the architecture needs stronger evidence and auditability
- references are scattered across notes, meetings, and systems
- review packs and documentation need a reliable evidence trail

## Inputs

- source documents, meeting outputs, links, and architecture artifacts
- known rationale and supporting references

## Outputs

- curated reference sets linked to architecture artifacts
- stronger provenance and source-of-truth coverage
- better support for review, audit, and decision traceability

## Typical Collaboration

- works closely with `business-analyst` and `architecture-governance-lead`
- complements `review-pack-builder`, `communication-packager`, and `artifact-maintainer`
